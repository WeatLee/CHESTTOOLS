Modification: 18 bronchopulmonary segment OBJ meshes were merged into five lung lobes. Heart, aorta, carotid and jugular vessels, pulmonary arteries, and pulmonary veins were grouped from the same source; the descending aorta was trimmed and capped to the displayed chest region. The meshes were scaled, reoriented, and compressed for the educational viewer. A simplified left common carotid continuation is drawn in code. The modified BodyParts3D geometry remains under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP.

Procedure model geometry: The trachea, main bronchi and segmental bronchial trees, esophagus, azygos and hemiazygos veins, diaphragm, vagus and left phrenic nerves, left common carotid and both subclavian artery trunks, both subclavian veins, ribs, costal cartilages, sternum, C7 to L1 vertebrae, clavicles, scapulae, and skin are modified from BodyParts3D version 4.3 (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP). They were scaled and reoriented into the lung coordinates, simplified by vertex clustering, and the skin was clipped to the chest. Bronchoscope routes were traced through a voxelized copy of the airway. Azygos vertices overlapping the airway, vertebrae or the drawn aorta were displaced outward. Lymph node stations are schematic positions computed beside the airway. The right phrenic nerve is a schematic path drawn in code. The modified geometry remains under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP.
